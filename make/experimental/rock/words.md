## Where And When

```text
in
out

up
down

front
back

over
under

before
after

now
then
```

Rock has no relation form, so these are ordinary words. `in` is a
thing you do, not a link between two nouns. Tune Code's `-e` relation
form came from exactly these words being said over and over in series
until they wore down into a suffix.

## Saying No

```text
no
not
gone
lack
```

Rock has no operator form either. Negation is a word that sits in the
sentence like any other. Tune Code's `-o` came from these wearing down
the same way.

## What Rock Does Not Have

```text
# tune code's words.md carries all of these
# rock carries none of them

if / then
and / or
of
the / a
question

pattern / instance
variable
function
recursion
type
state

energy
frequency
dimension
axis
polarity as a named thing

model
interpret
predict

union / intersection
threshold
constraint

any word for a number past three
any word for an idea about an idea
```

Rock can say what is, what happens, and what something is like. It
cannot say *if*, *or*, *of*, or *not* as grammar rather than as a word.
Every one of those is a Tune Code invention, and that is why Rock was
replaced.
