```text
tune rock has nine sounds

they are the nine a body makes with nothing but itself

three vowels
two hums
three hits
one breath

that is the whole language
```

## The Vowels

```text
i
a
u

three heights

i = high, out, bright, hot, sun
a = level, surface, warm, earth
u = low, inner, dark, cold, moon

this is the only axis rock has

i -> a -> u

above -> present -> within

a vowel is not a thing
it is a level
```

Three vowels sit as far apart as three vowels can. That is why the
system is stable and why a child can learn it. Tune Code later split
`i` down to `e` and `u` down to `o`, and gained precision at the cost
of that distance.

## The Hum

```text
m
n

the two sounds you can hold with your mouth closed

m = good
n = bad

m = toward, warm, nourishing, mother, home
n = away, cold, refusing, alone, edge

this is polarity itself

it is the first thing an animal needs to say
and it is the only pair in rock that is easy to confuse

rock does not avoid that
rock uses it

mata and nata are meant to sit that close
because good and bad sit that close
```

Tune Code kept the pair and grew it. `m` opened into `w`, and `n` went
back into `q` and loosened into `l` and `r`. The negative hum was the
more productive of the two.

## The Beat

```text
p
t
k

the three places you can stop the air and let it go

p = lips, the front, the near
t = tongue, the middle, the step
k = throat, the back, the far

these are the drum

p is the low hit
t is the tick
k is the crack

k is a rock chipping
the sound of one stone struck on another
the first tool
the shape bouncing off a flat surface

this is why the language is called rock

you can beat these three with your mouth alone
and rock is chanted, so that is what it is built on

p = touch
t = step
k = strike
```

Voicing did not exist yet. `b`, `d` and `g` are what happened to `p`,
`t` and `k` when the hum leaked into them. Frication did not exist
either. `f`, `s` and `x` are what happened when the stop stopped
closing all the way. Every one of Tune Code's obstruents is one of
these three hits, softened.

## The Breath

```text
h

air with nothing in the way

h = breath
h = the life in the body
h = the edge of a word

h does not sit in the middle of anything

it opens a word
or it carries the role at the end

so in a chanted stream with no pauses
every h is a boundary

that is what lets rock be sung
and still be understood
```

The breath carries the three roles, `ha`, `hi` and `hu`. When Tune
Code dropped it, those became `-a`, `-i` and `-u`, and the breath
survived only where it had been at the front of a word.

## The Whole Body

```text
vowels    i a u        the head, what level a thing is at
hum       m n          the chest, whether it is good or bad
beat      p t k        the hands, front and middle and far
breath    h            the lungs, the edge, the life

a word is

  a level
  a polarity
  a strike
  and the breath around it
```


The meanings above are not fixed. They are what the sounds want. Rock
is small enough that a root can only mean so many things, and the
lexicon is filled in by carrying Tune Code backwards, not by choosing
each root by hand. See `fold.ts`.

## Why Nine

```text
nine is what a body has

two hums, because there are two directions a feeling goes
three hits, because there are three places to stop the air
one breath, because there is one set of lungs
three levels, because there is up and level and down

nothing here was chosen
it is what is left when you take away everything that needs practice

and nine became twenty seven

three times nine

the same body, told apart three ways over
```
