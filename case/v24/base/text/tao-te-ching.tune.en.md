# 道德經, in Tune

A gloss, not a text. **The 51 closed words and every content root are
written in English**, because neither has been assigned a form yet, so
what is being tested here is the STRUCTURE.

Written 2026-09-20 against `note/tune/grammar/reference.md`, from the
Classical Chinese in `tao-te-ching.zh.md`, with the Wikisource English
in `tao-te-ching.en.md` for sense. **`tao-te-ching.tune.md` is this
text in v16 forms**, rendered from this file by
`pnpm --dir deck/tune v16:book`: the grammar is decided here and the
sounds are looked up there. **The Chinese is the source**,
because Classical Chinese is the one natural language built the way
Tune is built: isolating, one syllable a word, no inflection, a small
closed set of particles doing all the grammar. Every device it uses
has to land somewhere in the 51, or the 51 are wrong.

```text
-i  action        -a  object        -o  feature, as head
-e  relation      bare  modifier    +   inside one compound word
```

**Read the Chinese line, then the Tune line under it.** The English is
in the other file. Where a chapter needed a decision the grammar had
not made, a note follows it, and every one of those notes is collected
at the foot of the page. Punctuation is for a reader of English and is
not part of Tune.

**How the particles land**, so the eighty one chapters can be read
without re-deriving them:

| Chinese | Tune | what it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 之 | `of-e`, or a bare modifier | the genitive |
| 其 | `its` `their` | the possessive |
| 而 | `and-e` | and, yet, but |
| 故 是以 | `from-e this-a` | therefore, and 是以 says it the same way, by this |
| 夫唯 | `only from-e` | only because |
| 者 | a relative clause on `person-a` or `thing-a` | the one who |
| 所 | a relative clause with the gap in the object | what is V-ed |
| 可 | `can` | can |
| 常 恆 | `forever` | eternal, always |
| 無 | `not+exist-a`, `not have-i`, `not` | non-being, has no, not |
| 有 | `exist-i`, `have-i`, `exist-a` | there is, has, being |
| 無為 | `not+do-a` | non-action, one word |
| 莫 | `not one` | none, nobody |
| 皆 | `all` | all |
| 相 | `each+other-a` | each other |
| 若 如 似 猶 | `same ... from-e` | like, the equative |
| 曰 謂 | `call-i X-a Y-a` | is called, the verb first and the caller unsaid |
| 則 | `if-e ... ,` | then, carried by the condition |
| 乎 耶 邪 | `question` | the question particle |

## 上篇

### 1

> 道可道，非恆道；
> 名可名，非恆名。
> 無名，天地之始；
> 有名，萬物之母。
> 故，
> 恆無欲也，以觀其妙；
> 恆有欲也，以觀其徼。
> 此兩者同出而異名，
> 同謂之玄。
> 玄之又玄，眾妙之門。

```text
can say-i way-a not forever way-a.
can name-i name-a not forever name-a.
not+name-a start-a of-e sky-a and-e earth-a.
have+name-a mother-a of-e all thing-a.
from-e this-a,
forever not want-i, to-e see-i its wonder-a.
forever want-i, to-e see-i its edge-a.
this two-a come-i from-e same place-a and-e have-i other name-a.
call-i all two-a dark-a.
dark-a of-e dark-a gate-a of-e all wonder-a.
```

**`can say-i way-a not forever way-a` is the whole first line.** A
relative clause with no pronoun, `can say-i way-a`, the way one can
say, then a second object with `not` in front of it, and two objects
identify. 非 is not a copula and neither is anything in Tune.

**`call-i all two-a dark-a` is 謂之玄.** The verb comes first, the
caller is unsaid, and the two objects after it are the thing and its
name. See note one.

**`from-e this-a` is 故.** See note two.

### 2

> 天下皆知美之爲美，
> 斯惡已；
> 皆知善之爲善，
> 斯不善已。
> 故
> 有無相生，
> 難易相成，
> 長短相形，
> 高下相傾，
> 音聲相和，
> 前後相隨。
> 是以
> 聖人處無爲之事，
> 行不言之敎，
> 萬物作焉而不辭，
> 生而不有，
> 爲而不恃，
> 功成而弗居。
> 夫唯弗居，是以不去。

```text
all world-a know-i beauty-a beauty-o,
from-e this-a ugly-a exist-i.
all know-i good-a good-o,
from-e this-a not good-a exist-i.
from-e this-a
exist-a and-e not+exist-a make-i each+other-a,
hard-a and-e easy-a form-i each+other-a,
long-a and-e short-a shape-i each+other-a,
high-a and-e low-a lean-i to-e each+other-a,
tone-a and-e voice-a join-i each+other-a,
front-a and-e back-a follow-i each+other-a.
from-e this-a
wise person-a do-i work-a of-e not+do-a,
do-i teach-a of-e not say-i,
all thing-a rise-i and-e it-a not refuse-i,
make-i and-e not have-i,
do-i and-e not lean-i,
work-a complete-o and-e not stay-i at-e it-a.
only from-e not stay-i, from-e this-a it-a not go-i.
```

**`know-i beauty-a beauty-o` is 知美之爲美.** A complement clause,
"knows [that] beauty is beautiful", runs to the end by the extent rule.

**`exist-a and-e not+exist-a make-i each+other-a` is 有無相生.** 相 is
the reciprocal and it was already `each+other-a`.

### 3

> 不尙賢，
> 使民不爭。
> 不貴難得之貨，
> 使民不爲盜。
> 不見可欲，
> 使民心不亂。
> 是以聖人之治，
> 虛其心，
> 實其腹，
> 弱其志，
> 強其骨。
> 常使民無知無欲，
> 使夫智者不敢為也。
> 為無為，
> 則無不治。

```text
not honour-i able person-a,
make-i people-a not fight-i.
not value-i hard get-i thing-a,
make-i people-a not steal-i.
not show-i can want-i thing-a,
make-i people heart-a not mess-o.
from-e this-a wise person-a rule-i,
empty-i their heart-a,
fill-i their belly-a,
weak-i their will-a,
strong-i their bone-a.
forever make-i people-a not know-i and-e not want-i,
make-i clever person-a not dare-i do-i.
do-i not+do-a,
if-e this-o, not one thing-a not rule-o.
```

**`make-i people-a not fight-i` is 使民不爭.** The causative is an
ordinary verb taking a clause, "makes [that] the people do not fight",
the extent rule again.

**`empty-i their heart-a` is 虛其心.** Chinese turns the adjective into
a verb by position and Tune turns it by ending: `empty-o` is empty,
`empty-i` is to empty. **No derivation needed.**

**`if-e this-o, not one thing-a not rule-o` is 則無不治.** 則 is "then"
and Tune carries it inside the condition. The double negative reads as
written.

### 4

> 道沖，
> 而用之或不盈。
> 淵兮，似萬物之宗。
> 挫其銳，
> 解其紛，
> 和其光，
> 同其塵。
> 湛兮，似或存。
> 吾不知誰之子，
> 象帝之先。

```text
way-a empty-o,
and-e use-i it-a and-e it-a not ever fill-o.
deep-o, same-o from-e root-a of-e all thing-a.
blunt-i its sharp-a,
loose-i its knot-a,
soft-i its light-a,
join-i its dust-a.
still-o, same exist-i from-e thing-a.
I-a not know-i it-a child-a of-e what+person-a,
it-a more early exist-i from-e god-a.
```

**`same-o from-e root-a of-e all thing-a` is 似萬物之宗.** The equative
as a bare predicate, "is the same as the root of all things", and the
standard on `from`. Every 若, 如, 似 and 猶 in the book is this.

**`more early exist-i from-e god-a` is 象帝之先.** Exists earlier than
god: the comparative on the verb and the standard on `from`. A first
draft wrote `before-e god-a` and the checker refused it, because `-e`
is on ten words and `before` is not one. See note three.

### 5

> 天地不仁，
> 以萬物為芻狗；
> 聖人不仁，
> 以百姓為芻狗。
> 天地之閒其猶橐籥乎？
> 虛而不屈，
> 動而愈出。
> 多言數窮，
> 不如守中。

```text
sky-a and-e earth-a not kind-o,
hold-i all thing-a straw dog-a.
wise person-a not kind-o,
hold-i people-a straw dog-a.
question space-a at-e middle-a of-e sky-a and-e earth-a same-o from-e bellows-a?
empty-o and-e not fail-i,
move-i and-e more out come-i.
many say-i and-e near end-i,
inverse more good-o from-e hold-i middle-a.
```

**`hold-i all thing-a straw dog-a` is 以萬物為芻狗.** "Holds all things
[as] straw dogs": two objects after the verb, the thing and what it is
taken for, which is the same shape as `call-i X-a Y-a`.

**`inverse more good-o from-e hold-i middle-a` is 不如守中.** Less good
than holding the middle. The inferior comparative, and the standard is
a clause.

> 谷神不死，是謂玄牝。
> 玄牝之門，是謂天地根。
> 綿綿若存，用之不勤。

valley spirit-a not die-i, call-i this-a dark female-a.
gate-a of-e dark female-a, call-i this-a root-a of-e sky-a and-e earth-a.
same continue exist-i from-e thread-a, use-i it-a and-e it-a not tire-o.

### 7

> 天長地久。
> 天地所以能長且久者，
> 以其不自生，
> 故能長生。
> 是以聖人後其身
> 而身先，
> 外其身
> 而身存。
> 非以其無私邪？
> 故能成其私。

```text
sky-a long-o and-e earth-a last-o.
cause-a of-e sky-a and-e earth-a can long-o and-e last-o,
it-a from-e their not live-i to-e self-a,
from-e this-a they-a can long live-i.
from-e this-a wise person-a back put-i their body-a
and-e their body-a front-o,
out put-i their body-a
and-e their body-a exist-i.
question not from-e their not have-i self-a?
from-e this-a they-a can achieve-i their self-a.
```

**`cause-a of-e sky-a and-e earth-a can long-o and-e last-o` is
所以...者.** "The cause of [that] sky and earth can be long and
lasting": `of` takes a clause by the reach rule, and the clause is the
thing whose cause is asked.

**`back put-i their body-a` is 後其身.** 後 is a noun used as a verb,
"to back one's body", and Tune puts the direction on the verb as a
bare modifier, the way the rabbit story wrote `in breathe-i`. A first
draft wrote `back-e` and was refused. See note three.

**`achieve-i` is 成.** `complete` is an aspect word and cannot be a
verb, and the checker said so. Every 成 and 果 on the page is
`achieve-i`.

### 8

> 上善若水。
> 水善利萬物而有靜，
> 處眾人之所惡，
> 故幾於道。
> 居善地，
> 心善淵，
> 與善仁，
> 言善信，
> 正善治，
> 事善能，
> 動善時。
> 夫唯不爭，
> 故無尤。

```text
most good-a same-o from-e water-a.
water-a good help-i all thing-a and-e still-o,
stay-i at-e all person-a hate-i place-a,
from-e this-a it-a close-o to-e way-a.
stay-i good at-e ground-a,
heart-a good at-e deep-a,
give-i good at-e kind-a,
say-i good at-e true-a,
rule-i good at-e order-a,
work-i good at-e able-a,
move-i good at-e time-a.
only from-e not fight-i,
from-e this-a not blame-a exist-i.
```

**`all person-a hate-i place-a` is 眾人之所惡.** The place everybody
hates: a relative clause with the gap in the object, no pronoun, and
`place-a` fills the gap. 所 does exactly this in Chinese.

**`stay-i good at-e ground-a` is 居善地.** Seven lines of three
characters each, verb, `good`, and where the good lies. Tune keeps the
shape: verb, bare `good` modifying nothing in particular, and `at-e`.
`good` is bare and something follows it, so the checker passes it,
though it reads as an adverb on the verb rather than the noun. The
Chinese is that terse too.

### 9

> 持而盈之，不如其已。
> 揣而銳之，不可常保。
> 金玉滿堂，莫之能守。
> 富貴而驕，自遺其咎。
> 功遂身退，天之道。

```text
hold-i and-e fill-i it-a, inverse more good-o from-e stop-i it-a.
beat-i and-e sharp-i it-a, not can forever keep-i it-a.
gold-a and-e jade-a fill-i hall-a, not one person-a can keep-i it-a.
rich-o and-e high-o and-e proud-o, self-a leave-i its blame-a.
work-a complete-o and-e body-a back go-i, this-a way-a of-e sky-a.
```

**`not one person-a can keep-i it-a` is 莫之能守.** 莫 is "none" and
it is `not one`. `not some` is the reference's form and `not one` reads
better before a noun. Both are derived and neither is a word.

> 載營魄抱一，
> 能無離乎？
> 專氣致柔，
> 能嬰兒乎？
> 滌除玄覽，

> 能無爲乎？
> 生之畜之，
> 生而不有，
> 為而不恃，
> 長而不宰，
> 是謂玄德 。

```text
carry-i body-a and-e soul-a and-e hold-i one-a,
question can not part-i?
gather-i breath-a and-e reach-i soft-a,
question can same-o from-e baby-a?
wash-i and-e clean-i dark mirror-a,
question can not have-i flaw-a?
love-i people-a and-e rule-i land-a,
question can not know-i?
sky gate-a open-i and-e close-i,

make-i it-a and-e feed-i it-a,
make-i and-e not have-i,
do-i and-e not lean-i,
grow-i and-e not rule-i,
call-i this-a dark virtue-a.
```

**`question can not part-i?` is 能無離乎.** Subjectless, and the
question word first, the modal, the negation, the verb. Six lines have
the shape and all six pass.

### 11

> 三十輻共一轂，
> 當其無，有車之用。
> 埏埴以為器，
> 當其無，有器之用。
> 鑿戶牖以為室，
> 當其無，有室之用。
> 故有之以為利，
> 無之以為用。

```text
three+ten spoke-a share-i one hub-a,
at-e its not+exist-a, use-a of-e cart-a exist-i.
shape-i clay-a to-e make-i pot-a,
at-e its not+exist-a, use-a of-e pot-a exist-i.
cut-i door-a and-e window-a to-e make-i room-a,
at-e its not+exist-a, use-a of-e room-a exist-i.
from-e this-a exist-a give-i gain-a,
not+exist-a give-i use-a.
```

**`at-e its not+exist-a` is 當其無.** "At its nothing", where the
nothing is. 無 as a thing is `not+exist-a`, one compound, and it takes
a possessive like any thing.

### 12

> 五色令人目盲，
> 五音令人耳聾，
> 五味令人口爽，
> 馳騁畋獵令人心發狂，
> 難得之貨令人行妨。
> 是以聖人為腹不為目，
> 故去彼取此。

```text
five colour-a make-i person eye-a blind-o,
five tone-a make-i person ear-a deaf-o,
five taste-a make-i person mouth-a numb-o,
ride-i and-e hunt-i make-i person heart-a wild-o,
hard get-i thing-a make-i person act-a wrong-o.
from-e this-a wise person-a work-i to-e belly-a and-e not to-e eye-a,
from-e this-a leave-i that-a and-e take-i this-a.
```

**`leave-i that-a and-e take-i this-a` is 去彼取此.** The
demonstratives as things, this one and that one, which the rabbit
story found and the Chinese does constantly.

### 13

> 寵辱若驚，
> 貴大患若身。
> 何謂寵辱若驚？
> 寵為下。
> 得之若驚，失之若驚，
> 是謂寵辱若驚。
> 何謂貴大患若身？
> 吾所以有大患者，為吾有身；
> 及吾無身，吾有何患？
> 故貴以身為天下，
> 若可寄天下。
> 愛以身為天下，
> 若可託天下。

```text
favour-a and-e shame-a same-o from-e fear-a,
value-i big trouble-a same-o from-e body-a.
what-a mean-i favour-a and-e shame-a same-o from-e fear-a?
favour-a low-o.
get-i it-a same-o from-e fear-a, lose-i it-a same-o from-e fear-a,
call-i this-a favour-a and-e shame-a same-o from-e fear-a.
what-a mean-i value-i big trouble-a same-o from-e body-a?
I-a have-i big trouble-a from-e I-a have-i body-a.
if-e I-a not have-i body-a, I-a have-i what trouble-a?
from-e this-a value-i world-a same-o from-e body-a,
if-e this-o, can give-i world-a to-e it-a.
love-i world-a same-o from-e body-a,
if-e this-o, can trust-i world-a to-e it-a.
```

**`what-a mean-i ...` is 何謂.** The question word where the answer
goes, the subject slot, and what follows is quoted. Tune has no
quotation marks and the extent rule takes the rest as the complement.

**`I-a have-i what trouble-a?` is 吾有何患.** `what` bare, modifying
`trouble-a`, in the object slot. Nothing moves.

### 14

> 視之不見，名曰夷；
> 聽之不聞，名曰希；
> 摶之不得，名曰微。
> 此三者不可致詰，故混而為一。
> 其上不皦，其下不昧，
> 繩繩不可名，復歸於無物，
> 是謂無狀之狀，無物之象，
> 是謂惚恍。
> 迎之不見其首，隨之不見其後。
> 執今之道以御今之有，能知古始，
> 是謂道紀。

```text
look-i at-e it-a and-e not see-i, call-i it-a flat-a.
listen-i to-e it-a and-e not hear-i, call-i it-a thin-a.
grasp-i it-a and-e not get-i, call-i it-a small-a.
this three-a not can ask-i to-e end-a, from-e this-a mix-i and-e one-o.
its top-a not bright-o, its under-a not dark-o,
continue continue-o and-e not can name-i, back go-i to-e not+thing-a,
call-i this-a shape-a of-e not+shape-a, form-a of-e not+thing-a,
call-i this-a dim-a and-e blur-a.
meet-i it-a and-e not see-i its head-a, follow-i it-a and-e not see-i its back-a.
hold-i way-a of-e now-a to-e drive-i exist-a of-e now-a, can know-i old start-a,
call-i this-a thread-a of-e way-a.
```

**`continue continue-o` is 繩繩.** Reduplication for intensity, and
Tune has no reduplication rule. Written as a modifier on itself, which
the checker passes and the grammar has not ruled on. See note four.

### 15

> 古之善為道者，微妙
> 玄達，深不可識。
> 夫唯不可識，故強為之容。
> 豫兮，若冬涉川；
> 猶兮，若畏四鄰；
> 儼兮，其若客；
> 渙兮，若冰之將釋；
> 敦兮，其若樸；
> 曠兮，其若谷；
> 渾兮，其若濁。
> 孰能濁以靜之徐清。
> 孰能安以動之徐生。
> 保此道不欲盈。
> 夫唯不盈，故能蔽而新成。

```text
old good do-i way-a person-a, small wonder-o,
dark reach-o, deep-o and-e not can know-o.
only from-e not can know-o, from-e this-a force-i shape-a to-e it-a.
slow-o, same-o from-e cross-i river-a at-e winter-a.
wary-o, same-o from-e fear-i four neighbour-a.
grave-o, same-o from-e guest-a.
loose-o, same-o from-e ice-a near melt-i.
plain-o, same-o from-e raw wood-a.
open-o, same-o from-e valley-a.
mixed-o, same-o from-e muddy water-a.
what+person-a can still-i muddy-a to-e slow clear-o?
what+person-a can move-i rest-a to-e slow live-o?
keep-i this way-a and-e not want-i fill-i.
only from-e not fill-i, from-e this-a can wear-o and-e new complete-o.
```

**`old good do-i way-a person-a` is 古之善為道者.** Three modifiers
and a relative clause stacked on `person-a`: the old, good, way-doing
person. It parses, and it is the densest thing on the page.

### 16

> 致虛極；守靜篤。
> 萬物並作，

# What the book found

Eighty one chapters, and **every Tune line on this page was run
through `check.ts`**, 1,195 utterances, and none is refused. `pnpm
check:tune:story` from `mesh/` runs it again, with the rabbit story.
Every count below was measured on the Chinese, not remembered.

The Chinese was chosen as the source because it is the one natural
language built the way Tune is built, and the test was whether every
particle it uses lands somewhere in the 51. **Every one did.** Eleven
things are worth writing down. Four changed the checker, one changed a
rule, and six are recorded so the next reader does not re-derive
them.

## One. "Is called" is the verb first and the caller unsaid

是謂 appears twenty seven times, 曰 twenty two, and 謂 alone eight
more. There is no voice and no passive, so "this is called X" cannot
be marked on the verb.

```text
call-i this-a dark virtue-a       this is called dark virtue
call-i big-a go-a                 great is called passing
call-i know-i forever-a bright-a  knowing the eternal is called bright
```

**The verb comes first, the caller is unsaid, and the two objects
after it are the thing and its name.** A verb-initial clause has no
subject, so its first object is the patient, and the second is what
the patient is called. This is the same shape as the ditransitive
`give-i Mary-a book-a`, and it is what 謂 does in Chinese, where 是 is
the fronted object and the caller is nobody.

**Not new.** The rabbit story's `thing-a must do-i` is the object
first and the doer unsaid. This is the verb first and the doer unsaid.
Both are what "no voice" means.

## Two. 故 is `from-e this-a`, and it is the strongest case for a fifty second word

故 appears sixty seven times and 是以 thirty eight, a hundred and five
in eighty one chapters, and the Tune has `from-e this-a` a hundred and
twenty four times once 乃, 斯 and a causal 以 are counted. **"Therefore"
is said more than any other function word in the book**, and Tune says
it in two words, `from-e this-a`, from this.

The case for a word is the same case that made `we` and `my` words:
the most said words should be the shortest. The case against is that
**Classical Chinese itself says it both ways**, 故 in one syllable and
是以 in two, by-this, which is exactly `from-e this-a`, and the book
uses both freely. The two-word form is attested in the source
language, it is derived from the seven relations, and adding a word
for one connective is what rule 2 exists to refuse.

**Not adopted, and recorded as the open question it is.** If a
fifty second word is ever added, this is the one.

## Three. `-e` is on ten words and on nothing else

A first draft wrote `before-e god-a`, `after-e it-a`, `between-e sky-a
and-e earth-a`, `above-e it-a`, `come-i out-e`, `go-i back-e`. **The
checker passed every one**, because it had never been told that an
open root cannot take `-e`.

The reference is clear: there are seven relations, they are Pāṇini's,
and `if`, `and`, `or` are the other three words that take the ending.
An open root with `-e` is an eighth relation.

**Every one derives**, and Chinese derives them the same way:

```text
at-e front-a of-e X-a         before X          X之前
at-e back-a of-e X-a          after X           X之後
at-e middle-a of-e X-a        between X         X之間
at-e top-a of-e X-a           above X           X之上
out come-i                    comes out         出
back go-i                     goes back         復歸
more early exist-i from-e X-a exists before X   先X
```

**Adopted in the checker**, with a test. "Before" in time is the
comparative on the verb with the standard on `from`, which is what
the reference already said for "earlier than".

## Four. A closed word is never a verb

`complete-i` for 成, `near-i` for 親. The checker refused both, and it
was right: `complete` is an aspect and `near` is an aspect, and an
aspect word modifies. **Every 成 and 果 on the page is `achieve-i`
and 親 is `close-i`.** Content roots, both. And `near-o`, which the
checker tolerates because a modifier word may head as a feature,
would still mean "is imminent" rather than "is close", so 幾於道 and
孰親 take `close-o` as well.

Recorded because it is the commonest slip a translator makes, and
the checker holds it.

## Five. Topic and comment is bare juxtaposition, and `topic` went unused

The Tao Te Ching is the most topic-prominent text there is. 者...也
frames a definition, 兮 marks a topic pause, and the book sets a thing
and then says something about it on nearly every page.

```text
weapon-a tool-a of-e bad sign-a           兵者，不祥之器
harm-a, luck-a lean-i at-e it-a           禍兮福所倚
good person-a, I-a good-i it-a            善者吾善之
high-a, press-i it-a down-a               高者抑之
```

**Every one of them is a name and then a clause, with `it-a` pointing
back.** That is topic and comment, and it needed no word. **`topic-e`
was not used once in eighty one chapters**, and the rabbit story did
not use it either. The relation word has now sat through the two
texts most likely to need it and been reached for by neither.

**Not removed, and flagged.** The word may have a job in a sentence
where the topic is not the subject and not an object of anything,
which neither text produced. Until one does, it is the one member of
the 51 with no attested use.

**The one cost of bare topic** is the 者 clause whose object and head
are the same noun:

```text
know-i person-a person-a clever-o          知人者智
glad-o at-e kill-i person-a person-a       樂殺人者
```

The one who knows people, the one glad at killing people. It parses,
the relative clause ends on its object and the head follows, and a
reader has to look at it twice. Chinese has the same collision and
resolves it with 者. Tune resolves it by position, and the cost is
real and small.

## Six. "No" is `not-o`

非乎, is it not, is `question not-o`. **The reference said "no" was
`not` alone, and under the bare rule `not` alone cannot stand**,
because a phrase never ends on a bare word. So the answer takes the
ending: `not-o`, it is not so.

**Adopted.** The negator takes `-o` when it is the whole predicate,
and the reference table is corrected.

## Seven. The correlative comparative is a condition

```text
if-e it-a go-i more far-o, it-a know-i more few-o     其出彌遠，其知彌少
```

The further one goes, the less one knows. Every language finds this
hard: Sanskrit has yāvat and tāvat, Mandarin has 越...越, English has
a construction found nowhere else in its grammar. **Tune has no word
for it and says it as a condition with a comparative on each side.**
The correlation is lost and the entailment is kept.

**Recorded as a convention.** It came up once in eighty one chapters,
and once in a hundred lines of story, and one construction is not
worth a word.

## Eight. Reduplication is a root modifying itself

熙熙, 昭昭, 察察, 悶悶, 沌沌, 芸芸, 繩繩, 惵惵, 醇醇, 缺缺, 恢恢. Twenty
two doubled characters in the book, and these eleven are the intensive
ones, where the rest are a verb on its own object, 病病, 生生. Tune has
no reduplication rule.

```text
bright bright-o        昭昭       very bright
dull dull-o            悶悶       very dull
many many-o            芸芸       teeming
```

**Written as the root modifying itself, and the checker passes it**,
because a bare word followed by a head is a modifier. Whether it means
intensity is a question of sense and not of shape, and the grammar has
not ruled on it. Chinese, Malay, Japanese and Hebrew all use it and
all for roughly this.

**Adopted as a reading, not a rule.** `X X-o` is very X. It costs
nothing and it is attested in every language that has it.

## Nine. 者 is a relative clause and 所 is a relative clause

者, the one who, appears ninety one times. 所, what is V-ed, appears
twenty eight. **Both are the relative clause with no pronoun**, and the
only difference is where the gap sits:

```text
know-i person-a person-a       知人者       the one who knows people, gap in the subject
person-a fear-i thing-a        人之所畏     what people fear, gap in the object
army-a stay-i place-a          師之所處     where the army stays, gap in the locative
```

A hundred and nineteen relative clauses and not one needed a pronoun,
a marker, or a ruling. This was decided before either text and it is
the single best-tested thing in the grammar now. Six of them end on
the same noun as their head, `person-a person-a`, which is note five.

## Ten. The equative and the identity are different relations

若, 如, 似, 猶 are "like", the equative: sixty three in the book, of
which forty nine are similes and the rest are 若 as "if". 同於 is "one
with", identity, and there are six.

```text
most good-a same-o from-e water-a      上善若水     like water, the standard on from
way person-a same-o with-e way-a       道者同於道   one with the Way, the partner on with
```

**`same` is the same word in both and the relation tells them apart.**
As tall as Mary is `from`, the standard of a comparison. One with the
Way is `with`, the comitative. The reference had the first and the
book supplied the second.

## Eleven. The line count matches

**Chinese says the thing in the fewest words of any language, and
Tune matched it more often than not.**

```text
知者不言                know-i person-a not say-i                  4 : 4
道生一                  way-a make-i one-a                         3 : 3
曲則全                  if-e bent-o, whole-o                       3 : 3
我無為而民自化          I-a not do-i and-e people-a self-a change-i  7 : 7
為無為                  do-i not+do-a                              3 : 2
無為而無不為            not do-i and-e not one thing-a not do-o    6 : 9
```

The last is the one line where Tune is longer, and it is longer
because 無不為 is "no not do" and Tune says whose not-doing is meant.
Where the Chinese leaves a subject out, Tune leaves it out. Where the
Chinese uses one character for a noun and a verb, Tune uses one root
and two endings.

## What did NOT break

**No missing relation, no missing aspect, no construction that needed
a word the 51 do not have.** The things that carried the most weight:

- **the relative clause with no pronoun** handled ninety 者 and 所
  clauses without comment
- **the extent rule** handled every complement, every 使 causative
  and every 知 with a clause after it
- **`if-e` alone** handled every 則 and every 若, and 雖 was `even if`
  as decided
- **`from-e`** handled every 以 and 故 and 於 of cause, and every
  standard of comparison
- **the ending as derivation** handled every noun used as a verb, 不德
  as `not virtue-i`, 病病 as `sick-i sick-a`, 甘其食 as `sweet-i their
  food-a`, without a morpheme
- **`not one`** handled every 莫, twenty of them, and sixteen 無
  besides
- **`command not`** handled every 勿, and `even if-e` every 雖
- **`self-a`** handled every 自, and `self+so-a` is 自然
